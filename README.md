# AoE2 tournament scouting

One page: how strong each player in the event is, overall and on each of the
eleven maps, estimated from their ranked and lobby match history.

**[Open the page](https://danielsery.github.io/aoe2-scout/)**

48 players across 9 rosters. Every figure is on the **team-ladder** scale, so it
matches no profile page and is not comparable to a 1v1 rating.

## How to read it

- **`Avg` is the column to draft on** — a player's mean estimate across the eleven
  maps actually being played, which is not the same as how good they are in general.
- **`Own` is the row's health, and it comes before the average.** It counts how many
  of the eleven rest on that player's own record on that map. A `°` cell is the map
  *group* standing in and a `~` is the bare level — both are real estimates, but an
  average built on two measured maps and nine stand-ins is mostly one number repeated.
- **Do not quote a single player's number to better than a couple of hundred Elo, and
  do not read a single map cell as a fact.** The per-map machinery is the weak half of
  this: on four of five map classes its deviations are indistinguishable from noise.
  The overall levels are the part that holds up.

## Where the numbers come from

Ladder ratings from both boards, converted onto one scale, corrected for playing with
a fixed group, and then corrected again toward a head-to-head fit over **54 164 ranked
matches** that never reads any tracked player's own rating. The Method view in the page
walks through it.

The estimator is scored against that head-to-head fit as an independent arbiter, and
the honest summary is in the source repo: the level machinery agrees with it closely,
the map machinery mostly does not.

## Publishing an update

```powershell
.\publish.ps1              # regenerate from the analysis repo, commit, push
.\publish.ps1 -SkipBuild   # publish the page as it already stands
```

Generated by [`site.py`](https://github.com/DanielSery/Aoe2Planner) in the analysis
repo — `index.html` here is a build artifact and must not be hand-edited.

## About the data

Every player here is a sign-up for this event. Aliases, ladder ratings and match
histories are public on aoe2companion.com; the **estimates** are this project's own
inference and are not endorsed by the players. If you are on this page and would
rather not be, open an issue and you will be removed.
